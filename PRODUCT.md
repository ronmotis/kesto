## Users

People meeting KESTO for the first time, often in Dallas and the US, many of whom have never eaten biltong. They are shopping for a flavourful, high-protein snack for everyday life and for sharing. Secondary: people who already know biltong from Southern Africa and want a trusted source. Tertiary: wholesale/retail buyers who need an enquiry route.

## Product Purpose

An online storefront for KESTO's Southern African-style snacks: Original Biltong, Chilli Biltong, Chilli Bites and Magunanzi Trail Mix (roasted corn & nuts). It has to explain what biltong is, make buying easy on a phone, and carry the brand's three ideas: Southern African roots, everyday flavour, the pleasure of sharing.

## Positioning

Southern African biltong (seasoned beef, air-dried and sliced), not American jerky; plus Magunanzi, a roasted corn and nut mix. Brand line from packaging: "Crafted with passion, rooted in savory tradition."

## Product Principles

1. Explain before you sell: a newcomer should be able to say what biltong is after one scroll.
2. The shopping action is never more than a thumb away.
3. Packaging is shown as it really is.
4. Unverified claims are labelled until verified.
5. Food is for sharing; the brand voice is warm and generous, never macho.
