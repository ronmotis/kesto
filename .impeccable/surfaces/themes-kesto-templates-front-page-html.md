# Surface: KESTO homepage (front-page template)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: first-time snacker on a phone or desktop; job: understand biltong, pick a flavour, add to bag.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A sunny shared table, not a supplement shelf. The page refuses the category default of dark, macho "protein" packaging walls and leads with people laughing over a bowl of biltong; the black pouches are the only dark objects, so they pop.

OWN-WORLD: Cream ground (#FBEBD6), deep tomato display type (#A3120C) set huge in Chau Philomene One (stand-in for Nando's Hand), mango (#F5A511) for the action button and the benefit strip, a leaf-green (#2B4F0E) story band, red-and-white gingham and limes in photography, hand-drawn tomato/cream arrows that draw themselves. Labels are quiet Barlow.

STORY: Good food, good mood -> what's in it (benefits, provisional) -> pick a flavour (three pouches) -> what biltong is and where it comes from (source, craft, company) -> sharing is optional, good company isn't -> shop.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Cream header (red horn logo left, Shop / What's biltong? / Our roots / Bag right). Left 30%: four-line red headline "GOOD FOOD. GREAT MOOD." with mango underline stroke, two-line Barlow support, mango "Find my snack" button with a hand-drawn arrow curling into it. Right 70%: full-bleed warm photo of a woman laughing mid-bite at an outdoor table, a real KESTO Original pouch layered in her raised hand and a second standing on the gingham, "Pass it round!" hand note. Mango benefit strip closes the viewport at desktop.

FORM: Comp-led from the user's approved full-page mockup (.impeccable/reference/comp.png). seed: none (pinned) — "The attached full-page KESTO mockup is the approved creative direction."

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: hand-drawn arrows draw once on entry; the hero CTA arrow nudges toward the button on hover/focus. Motion grammar: short ease-out reveals, one mask reveal on the hero photo, staggered story photos, slight pouch depth.

Unresolved: Nando's Hand licence/file; real prices; Chilli Bites packshot; claims verification; founder/farm story; social URLs; hosting.

## Build deviations (pending user sign-off)

- Hero pouch "in her raised hand" (FIRST VIEWPORT): built as real pouch cut-outs standing on the table beside her, at about 18% of the photo width, instead of held. Reason: a generated pouch would corrupt the real label, and a pasted-in grip without a hand matte reads fake. A photoshoot (or an approved composite) with the real pouch in hand closes this. Approved by the user for now (2026-11-25); revisit after a photoshoot.
